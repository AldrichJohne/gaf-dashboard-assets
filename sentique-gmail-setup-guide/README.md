# Sentique Gmail authorization guide

Eleven numbered screens, in the supplied order. Personal email addresses, account avatar initials and exact expiry timestamps are covered with privacy blocks. Property and app names remain as examples.

## Disclaimer for the email and guide

Google's screens may look slightly different depending on your device, account and app updates. Follow the button labels shown in these steps. Some screens may be skipped if you have already granted access.

The unverified-app screens shown in steps 3 and 7 reflect the current testing stage and may disappear after Google verification. Continue only when you recognize Sentique, expected the setup invitation and trust the requested access.

Return to the original **Property setup** tab and click **Check connection** at the end. The helper's older success text mentions the dashboard, but this pre-login flow finishes on Property setup.
